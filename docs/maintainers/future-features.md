# Future product features

Ideas to consider after the current product wiring and reliability work. These are not part of the current release scope.

## Content editor: next capabilities

The project-scoped editor now has saved Markdown drafts, an SEO/AEO toggle, deterministic live checks and scores, plus an OpenRouter-backed writing assistant for outlines, first drafts, clarity improvements, rewrites, and FAQs. AI output stays in a review panel until the writer applies it. Future work to reach the broader Rank Math-style product brief:

- Analyze a supplied page or URL so writers can optimize content already published on a site.
- Ground AI suggestions in cited sources, factual research, and missing reader questions; writers must approve edits.
- Expand SEO checks to search intent, SERP context, readability, and contextual links. Expand AEO checks to citations, entity consistency, schema, and answer-engine evidence.
- Deliver approved fixes through copy-paste, a website snippet, and GitHub PRs for supported sites.
- Add revisions and recovery history before team collaboration or publishing workflows.

## Workspace and account settings

- **Crawler access:** configure access for audits of protected websites.
- **Account privacy and deletion:** provide data export/privacy controls and a complete account deletion flow.
- **Connection and setup status:** show whether Google, analytics/search trackers, and required services are connected.
- **Usage history:** show where credits were spent and explain failed operations; place this under billing.
