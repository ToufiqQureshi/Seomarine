About

SEO and AI search, made simple.

Uplumo is one tool for the two places customers now look for a business: Google, and AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude.

Why we built it

The big SEO tools are powerful, but most business owners use a small part of them and pay for all of it. Prices are in dollars, AI visibility is a paid add-on, and reports are capped by plan.

We built Uplumo around the complaints we hear most. Every screen has one clear next step. Every finding is explained in plain language, with a suggested fix. Prices are in rupees, and you can cancel in one click.

What it does

  • Uplumo Analytics. A lightweight, cookie-free tracker that shows your visitors, top pages and channels, with traffic from AI assistants called out separately.
  • AI Visibility. Brand mentions, cited sources and competitor share of voice across the major AI assistants.
  • Rank tracking and keywords. Track your keywords in Google and find new ones worth targeting.
  • Site audit. Crawl your site and see the issues that matter most first.
  • Client reports. White-label reports with your own logo, name and colours.

Who it is for

Solo owners who want to see what is working, and agencies who need to show results to clients.
