Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated {{.Updated}}

Cancelling a paid plan

  • You can cancel in one click from Billing, at any time. There are no calls and no forms.
  • After you cancel, you keep your paid plan until the end of the month you have paid for.
  • You are not charged again.
  • When the paid month ends, your workspace moves to the Free plan. Your data stays; if you have more than one active site, archive sites until you meet the Free plan limit.

Refunds

  • Paid plans are charged monthly in advance at the price shown on the pricing page. We do not refund part of a month after you cancel, because you keep the paid plan until that month ends.
  • If you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or charged by mistake, we refund that payment in full.
  • If the service did not work for you and we could not fix it, write to us and we will look at a refund for that month.

How to ask for a refund

Email {{.Support}} from your account's address within 7 days of the payment, with the date and amount.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show the money.
